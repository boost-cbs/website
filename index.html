The Challenge

What needs improving?

Cambridge Building Society has strong client relationships, but their digital services are not as easy and simple to use as they could be. The client-side interface needs to evolve so users get exactly what they need — quickly, intuitively, and without friction.

Complex Interfaces

Current systems require too many steps for basic tasks like checking balances or applying for products.

User Expectations

Members expect the same seamless digital experience they get from modern fintech apps.

Our Approach

How can this be done?

This transformation is accomplished through AI-powered tools that bridge the gap between complex back-end systems and effortless user experiences.

About Boost

What is Boost?

Boost is a strategic initiative to embed AI directly into Cambridge Building Society's client interfaces — making every interaction smarter, faster, and more personal.

Easier to Use

Streamlined interfaces that reduce cognitive load and guide users naturally.

Happier Members

Delightful experiences that increase satisfaction and long-term loyalty.

Simpler Financing

Transparent, jargon-free tools for mortgages, savings, and everyday banking.

Real Impact

How can it have an impact?

For mortgages and savings accounts, CBS's services must be effortless. Digital experiences at the highest level empower users to manage their finances with confidence.

When digital services are truly easy to use, member engagement and trust grow exponentially.
